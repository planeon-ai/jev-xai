# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

We pledge to make participation in jev-xai a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without permission

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through GitHub private vulnerability reporting, or by contacting the repository maintainers. Reports are reviewed and result in a response that the maintainers consider appropriate, including a warning or a ban from project spaces.
